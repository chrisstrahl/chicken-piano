# Future ideas

Ideas that came up but aren't planned yet.

- **Duets: the app plays the accompaniment.** While the child plays one hand (or the melody), Chicken Piano
  plays the other part in time, like a teacher sitting next to them. Chris liked the idea but put it off
  (2026-10-22). The pieces are mostly there: every song has a left-hand part and chords, and "play with
  the beat" already keeps time.
- **Chord lessons.** Lessons in the later levels that teach C, F and G chords before the block-chord songs.
- **Audio cable (line-in) input.** Pick a wired or USB audio input and use a cleaner signal profile. Low
  priority: the CT-S200's headphone jack mutes its speakers, and USB MIDI is the better path on iPad.
